Luma

Calories from a photo, food diary and your progress

A food diary with approximate calorie and macro estimates from a photo, weight tracking and an intermittent fasting timer.

Support

Questions and bug reports: rodionangeles@gmail.com. Include your iOS and Luma versions and describe the issue. Do not send medical documents, passwords or access tokens.

How do I correct an AI result?

Luma lists every food and drink in the photo separately. If it can't tell the size or version from the photo — for example a 12 oz or 16.9 oz bottle, regular or Zero — tap the right option and the numbers update instantly, without another scan. Use “Change amount” for your own portion or the ✕ button to remove an item. After saving, you can edit or delete each entry in the diary. AI estimates visible food and can be wrong, especially with oil, sauces, packaging and portion size.

Why is my weight not found in Apple Health?

Check that Apple Health has weight entries and that Luma is allowed to read body mass. If access is limited, the app may not receive entries. You can always enter your weight manually.

Where is my data and how do I delete it?

Your diary, weight and scan history are stored on your device. You can delete diary and scan entries in the app. The server analysis identifier is deleted in Goals → Privacy and Support. Deleting the app removes its local data but not Apple Health records; the identifier in the Keychain may remain after deleting the app.

Paid features

Version 1.0 is free, with no subscription or ads. Under heavy load or when a technical limit is reached, analysis may be temporarily unavailable. The manual diary works without AI.

Does Luma sync between iPhone and iPad?

Yes. With iCloud on and the same Apple ID, your diary, weight, goals, water and fasting sync automatically. Analysis history with photos stays on each device.

Units

Luma uses pounds, feet and fluid ounces in the U.S. and metric units elsewhere. You can switch units in Goals → Units of measure.

Important information

Luma is intended for adults and is not a medical app. Calories and fasting stages are general guidance. The app does not measure glucose, autophagy or fat burning. For medical questions, talk to a professional.
